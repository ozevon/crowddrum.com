Waitlist privacy notice

CrowdDrum is a product by Ozevon. This notice covers this marketing website and its launch-update waitlist, not a future CrowdDrum application.

What you share

The waitlist asks for your email address. Submitting it asks Ozevon to send you CrowdDrum launch updates. We do not ask for your payment details or create a product account.

How the form works

Your email address passes through FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, on its way to Ozevon's inbox at contact@ozevon.com. The form uses FormSubmit's spam protection, which may include a CAPTCHA. These services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the form. See FormSubmit's privacy policy for its handling of information.

What this site does

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and form providers may process technical information, such as your IP address, to serve and protect their services. This page does not include advertising trackers or analytics code, and it does not store your email in your browser.

Launch updates and removal

Ozevon uses your waitlist email for CrowdDrum launch updates. To stop receiving them or ask for removal of your waitlist email from Ozevon's records, write to contact@ozevon.com.
